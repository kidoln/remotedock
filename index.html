🔧 Troubleshooting

Cannot discover Mac?
Ensure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, check if Remote Dock for Mac is running, and verify Mac firewall settings.
Cannot paste to Mac?
Grant Accessibility permission on Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → Enable Remote Dock
Clipboard not syncing?
Confirm clipboard sync is enabled on Mac, check connection status, and try reconnecting.
